About Jebwach Travel Safari

Connecting travellers with the landscapes, wildlife and cultures of Africa.

OUR STORY

Travel With Purpose

Jebwach Travel Safari is a travel and safari brand focused on helping travellers discover the beauty and diversity of East Africa.

Our aim is to help individuals, families and groups explore destinations through wildlife safaris, cultural encounters, nature experiences and relaxing holidays.

Every journey should be planned with care, clear communication and respect for the places and communities travellers visit.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Mission, Vision and Values

Our Mission

To help travellers plan meaningful and memorable journeys through Africa by providing attentive service, useful travel information and experiences suited to their interests.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted travel brand known for thoughtful safari planning, memorable journeys and appreciation of Africa's natural and cultural heritage.

Our Values

Integrity, customer care, respect for local communities, responsible travel, transparency and a commitment to quality.
